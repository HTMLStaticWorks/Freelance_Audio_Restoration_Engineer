How it works

My Restoration Process

1. Secure Upload

Upload your audio files in high-resolution WAV, FLAC, or MP3 via our client portal.

2. Spectral Diagnostic

I analyze the audio's spectrogram to isolate specific noise frequencies and assess damage.

3. Surgical Restore

I run precise spectral repair passes, removing noise transients while keeping the target signal natural.

4. Final Master Delivery

Receive the restored high-fidelity audio masters, fully balanced and ready for distribution.
