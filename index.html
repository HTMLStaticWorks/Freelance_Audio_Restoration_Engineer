Professional Profile

Rescuing the World's Lost & Damaged Audio Heritage

I'm a freelance audio restoration engineer and spectral repair specialist. Whether you have a valuable music master reel suffering from decades of tape decay, a legal forensic recording that is barely audible, or a podcast dialogue tracking background noise, I surgically clean every frequency layer to restore absolute clarity.

My Story

My journey into audio restoration started 15 years ago while digitizing historic acetate discs for a national broadcasting archive. I was captivated by the challenge of removing severe surface crackle without erasing the emotional performances underneath. Since then, I've transitioned from general music production to specialized spectral analysis, combining classical audio sensibilities with modern machine-learning models.

Surgical Process

How I Restore Your Audio

01
Spectral Diagnostic

We conduct an initial FFT noise-profile assessment to map clicks, hums, and phase anomalies.

02
Surgical Extraction

Using spectral repair tools, I isolate and remove target artifacts without degrading adjacent signals.

03
Phase & Speed Calibration

I recalibrate transient alignment, time-delay errors, and wow & flutter fluctuations on tape transfers.

04
High-Fidelity Master

I run final tonal balancing and loudness sweeps to deliver crystal-clear, standard-compliant masters.
