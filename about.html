About Alex Reed

Freelance audio restoration specialist dedicated to the art and science of spectral repair.

My Mission & Background

My freelance practice is centered on a singular commitment: to salvage damaged recordings, historical archives, and forensic evidence that other studios deem unrecoverable. I treat every audio track with meticulous care and mathematical precision.

I combine classical critical listening sensibilities with cutting-edge spectral subtraction models. Unlike generalist mastering labs, I focus exclusively on audio archaeology and repair — isolating and removing hums, hiss, crackle, and clicks while keeping dialogue and performances sounding natural.

My Philosophy

Professional Standards

Every signal processed in my laboratory is subjected to the highest standards of technical verification, preserving authenticity and security.

Sonic Authenticity

I believe in preserving the original context of the performance. My restoration attenuates noise without coloring or gating the underlying dialogue.

NDA & Security

From evidence recordings to unreleased commercial music, I maintain strict chain-of-custody protocols and secure data transfers for all project assets.

Technical Precision

I utilize state-of-the-art spectral algorithms and custom tape transfers to ensure the highest resolution recovery possible.

Restoration Studio

Flat Acoustic Diagnostics

You cannot fix what you cannot hear accurately. My personal restoration studio features custom acoustic diffusion and calibrated monitoring to provide flat, uncolored frequency responses for surgical work.

  • PMC & ATC reference monitoring arrays
  • Studer & Ampex calibrated tape decks
  • Prism Sound reference converters
  • iZotope RX Advanced spectral suites
